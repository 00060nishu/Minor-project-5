# Excel Minor Project – Sales Operations Analytics

An **Excel-based Sales Operations Analytics project** designed to analyze retail sales data and answer real-world business questions.

## 📌 Project Overview

The project uses multiple datasets including **Orders, Customers, Products, Sales Representatives, and Targets** to perform data analysis and derive meaningful business insights.

## 🛠️ Skills & Excel Techniques

* Data Cleaning & Quality Checks
* VLOOKUP & INDEX-MATCH
* SUMIFS, COUNTIFS & AVERAGEIFS
* Pivot Tables & Data Aggregation
* RANK, LARGE & TEXTJOIN
* Running Totals & Top-N Analysis
* Referential Integrity & Duplicate Detection
* Multi-sheet Data Analysis

## 📊 Analysis Covered

* Customer and product lookups
* Data quality and duplicate analysis
* Revenue and order aggregation
* Sales representative performance
* Regional and category-wise sales analysis
* Quarterly and monthly revenue trends
* Top-performing products and sales representatives

## 📁 Workbook Structure

* `Orders` – Order transaction data
* `Customers` – Customer information
* `Products` – Product details
* `Sales_Reps` – Sales representative data
* `Targets` – Monthly sales targets
* `Q1_Lookups` – Lookup analysis
* `Q2_Cleaning` – Data cleaning & diagnostics
* `Q3_Aggregation` – Conditional aggregation
* `Q4_Pivot` – Pivot table analysis
* `Q5_Advanced` – Advanced Excel analysis

## 🎯 Objective

To demonstrate practical **Excel, data analysis, and business analytics skills** by transforming raw sales data into structured insights and answering business-oriented questions.
